WHO WE ARE

An in-house team, built to close the gap between design and delivery.

Trefina Interiors LLC was founded in 2020 to close the gap between design and delivery. Rather than handing a project between separate design firms, contractors and specialist trades, we built one in-house team covering civil works, MEP, custom joinery, metalwork, paint and flooring — so a client only ever has one point of contact, from first sketch to final handover.

Since then we've delivered fit-outs across the UAE's hospitality, commercial and residential sectors, for clients ranging from international hotel brands to national retailers — guided throughout by the same four principles: excellence, innovation, reliability and a client-driven approach.

WHAT WE DO

Six disciplines, one accountable team.

From the first concept sketch to the final coat of paint, every stage is handled in-house — so nothing gets lost in translation between trades.

Interior Design Consulting

From first idea to construction-ready design — layouts, materials and concepts refined with you at every step.

Civil Works

Structural and civil groundwork completed to the same standard as the finish that goes on top of it.

MEP & Electrical

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems, engineered, consulted on and installed by our own teams.

Custom Joinery & Metalwork

Bespoke joinery and precision metal fabrication, built in-house to fit the space exactly.

Paint, Tiling & Flooring

Precision paint and texture application, tiling, woodwork and expert flooring services.

Material & Furniture Procurement

Sourcing quality materials, furniture and accessories to complete the space, on brand and on budget.

HOW WE WORK

From brief to handover, in four stages.

The order matters — each stage is signed off before the next begins, so there are no surprises mid-build.

01

Consult

We walk the space, understand how it will be used, and scope the work against budget and timeline.

02

Design

Ideas are converted into construction-ready designs — materials, layouts and MEP planned before anything is built.

03

Build

Our own teams handle civil works, MEP, joinery, metalwork, paint and flooring — one crew, no subcontractor handoffs.

04

Handover

A final walkthrough, snagging resolved on the spot where possible, and documentation for everything installed.
